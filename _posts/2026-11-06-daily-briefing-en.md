## Compass Insight

The day after the election, stocks rallied on relief. The day after that, they repriced a new uncertainty. This looks less like a verdict on the election and more like a re-examination of how much AI optimism was already baked into prices, now that regulation is a live variable. With the Nasdaq in correction territory, the near-term question is whether this is a pause or the start of an unwind; the VIX, which jumped to about 27, and upcoming earnings are the tells. Over the medium term, watch what a Democratic Congress actually proposes on AI and data centers, and how oil responds to Iran headlines.
