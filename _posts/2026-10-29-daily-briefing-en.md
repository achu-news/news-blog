## Compass Insight

Today's thread is sticky inflation. The Fed stood still, but the dissents leaned hawkish, and September consumer prices were [up 3.7% from a year earlier](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/cpi-inflation-report-september-2026.html). With oil near $100 a barrel, fuel and food costs keep pressing on households. Short term, watch the Bank of Japan meeting and how the big-tech earnings reaction carries into Asian trading. Over the medium term, two things decide the direction of stocks, the dollar and mortgage rates: whether the Fed turns to hikes, and whether oil eases. This is analysis, not advice.
