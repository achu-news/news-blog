## Compass Insight

Friday looked like the day the market started asking for receipts. Stocks that had run on AI optimism were suddenly judged on cash flow and guidance, and the verdict was harsh. Yet money did not flee everything: energy and health care held up, and Exxon and Chevron beat estimates. The mix of 3.6% inflation, oil above $100 and debate over another rate hike is awkward for richly valued tech, because higher rates shrink the value of far-off profits. In the short run, watch Apple's earnings on Monday and any shift in December rate-hike expectations. Over the medium term, the question is whether AI spending produces visible revenue, and whether oil cools. This is analysis, not advice.
