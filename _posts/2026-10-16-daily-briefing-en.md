## Compass Insight

Thursday's tape told a two-sided story. In Tokyo, fear about how much debt is financing the AI build-out hit chipmakers hard; in New York, the same fear was cushioned by falling Treasury yields, which pushed cash toward homebuilders, retailers and other "non-AI" corners. That is a rotation, not a rout — but it shows how crowded the AI trade has become. In the short term, watch whether CoreWeave's bonds stabilize and whether yields keep easing into the Fed meeting. Over the longer run, the real test is earnings: AI spending has to start showing up as profits, not just capex.
