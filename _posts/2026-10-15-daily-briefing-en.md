## Compass Insight

One line connects the day's headlines: Gulf tensions push oil up, oil pushes gasoline up, and gasoline pushes U.S. inflation back toward 4%. That chain is why a "hot" CPI print hit growth stocks hardest while the Dow barely moved. In the short term, watch whether futures markets keep pricing an October hike; estimates of the odds ranged widely after the report, but all rose sharply from about one in five the day before. Over the longer run, the key variable is not the Fed but the Strait of Hormuz: if shipping attacks ease and oil retreats, inflation pressure could fade without further tightening. Until then, higher yields and a firm dollar are likely to keep volatility elevated in tech-heavy portfolios.
