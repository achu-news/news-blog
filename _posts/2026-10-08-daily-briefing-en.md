  *Why it matters:* The AI build-out is pulling in imports even as new tariff rounds take effect.
- **Jay Clayton named AI czar.** President Trump tapped the Director of National Intelligence to [lead a new White House AI task force](https://www.npr.org/2026/10/04/nx-s1-5990781/jay-clayton-ai-czar-trump) due to report within 120 days.

## Compass Insight

The story of the week is the price of money. Stocks sit near records on AI-fueled earnings hopes, yet the bond market keeps pushing back: oil near $100 and a hot AI investment cycle are keeping inflation risks tilted to the upside, and the Fed's own minutes confirm a bias toward tightening. Wednesday's session was telling — the headline indexes slipped only modestly while roughly two-thirds of stocks fell and small caps dropped more than 1%. In the near term, watch whether the 10-year can hold above 5.3% and how the Fed frames its late-October meeting. Longer term, the key question is whether AI-driven productivity arrives fast enough to justify both today's valuations and today's rates.
