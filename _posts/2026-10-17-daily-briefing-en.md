## Compass Insight

This week's market ran on one chain reaction: expensive oil keeps inflation sticky, sticky inflation keeps yields high, and high yields punish companies that borrowed heavily to build AI capacity. Thursday's relief rally on lower yields lasted a single session. Energy stocks were the lone bright spot, a reminder that the same force hurting consumers is rewarding producers. In the short run, watch whether the Fed hikes again at its [Oct. 27–28 meeting](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm) and whether Brent holds above $100. Over the longer run, the key question is whether AI spending turns into enough profit to service the debt behind it — earnings season is where that answer starts to show up.
