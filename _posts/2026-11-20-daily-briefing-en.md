## Compass Insight

This was a day when good news did not get bought. Anthropic's debut was the market's biggest AI event of the year, yet stocks still closed near their lows. That hints that the debate has shifted from "how big is AI?" to "can earnings keep growing when rates stay high?" With a December hike almost fully priced, the key short-term markers are the next inflation readings and whether dollar-yen can hold below the 160 level that Tokyo has defended.
