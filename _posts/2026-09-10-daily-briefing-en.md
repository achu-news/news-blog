## Compass Insight

The through-line today is oil. When fighting spreads across the Middle East, traders price in scarcity before any barrels actually go missing, and that front-running is why crude can spike double digits in days. For U.S. households, the transmission is direct: pump prices and utility bills follow crude with a lag of a few weeks. The knock-on effect is on rates. If energy keeps headline inflation sticky, expectations for another Fed hike can strengthen, Treasury yields can stay elevated, and mortgage and borrowing costs can remain high — which is exactly what equity markets were repricing this week. Near term, watch shipping activity through the Strait of Hormuz and whether Washington and Tehran find an off-ramp. Over the medium term, watch whether a $100 Brent handle sticks; that single number will shape the inflation and rate outlook into year-end. None of this is a call to trade — it is a map of what to watch.
