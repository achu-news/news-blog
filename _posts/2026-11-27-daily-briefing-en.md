## Compass Insight

Wednesday's session showed where market anxiety sits: not in earnings, but in whether AI spending will ever pay for itself. A 4.6% drop followed by a near-full recovery in a single day means sellers and bargain hunters are evenly matched, and that usually signals more volatility, not calm. Meanwhile sticky inflation limits how much help investors can expect from the Fed. In the short term, watch the December Fed meeting and any news on how AI infrastructure is being financed. Over the medium term, the question is whether AI investment turns into profit, and whether energy prices stay high because of the Iran conflict. This is analysis, not a forecast.
