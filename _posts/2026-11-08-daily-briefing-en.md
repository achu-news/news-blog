Stocks first cheered the election outcome, with the Dow up about 3% on Wednesday, then turned to the second-order effect: possible limits on data center buildout. Friday's jobs report added a second pressure point, because a hot labor market makes a December hike more likely just as growth stocks look fragile.

In the short run, watch [CPI on November 10 and PPI on November 13](https://blsmon1.bls.gov/schedule/2026/home.htm), and whether AI-linked shares stabilize or break lower. After sharp drops, volatility tends to stay high, so diversification matters more than timing.
