## Compass Insight

This week showed how a political result can reprice a whole market theme overnight. Stocks first cheered the election outcome, with the Dow up about 3% on Wednesday, then turned to the second-order effect: possible limits on data center buildout. Friday's jobs report added a second pressure point, because a hot labor market makes a December hike more likely just as growth stocks look fragile.

In the short run, watch next week's CPI and PPI readings and whether AI-linked shares stabilize or break lower. Over the medium term, the questions are how far the new Congress actually moves on data center rules, and whether valuations can hold while rates keep rising. After sharp drops, volatility tends to stay high, so diversification matters more than timing.
