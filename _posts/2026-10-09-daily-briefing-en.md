## Top U.S. Stories

- **AI stocks stumble on OpenAI revenue report.** The Financial Times reported that OpenAI told investors its annualized revenue was [approaching $50 billion at the end of September, well short of the roughly $70 billion previously reported](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/08/openai-50-billion-arr-anthropic-revenue-accounting). The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index closed down 3.39%.
  *Why it matters:* The gap reportedly reflects [a difference in how revenue is calculated, not lost sales](https://www.axios.com/2026/10/08/openai-50-billion-arr-anthropic-revenue-accounting) — but the selloff shows how sensitive richly valued AI stocks have become to any doubt about AI's real earning power.
- **Oil jumps back above $100.** Brent settled at [$104.28 and WTI at $91.49](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/oil-rises-as-middle-east-supply-concerns-persist-amid-shipping-attacks-ce785ddedf8af42d) as Iran stepped up attacks on tankers in the Strait of Hormuz and [U.S. media reported the Pentagon told Central Command to prepare for a possible resumption of "major combat operations"](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/08/oil-prices-are-climbing-on-concerns-about-an-escalating-iran-war).
  *Why it matters:* Higher crude feeds straight into gasoline and heating costs heading into winter, and keeps pressure on inflation and interest rates.
- **Trump rules out Iran strikes before midterms.** President Trump posted that ["we will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections"](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-nuclear-donald-trump-vance-rubio-strait-of-hormuz/) on November 3. Oil came off its session highs afterward.
- **Hurricane Isaias nears the Gulf Coast.** The first Atlantic hurricane of the season is [forecast to make landfall late Friday or early Saturday near Alabama and the Florida Panhandle](https://apnews.com/article/hurricane-isaias-gulf-atlantic-alabama-florida-mississippi-8244ca45b8d4c292350f00adfabb0967), with evacuations and school closures in coastal areas.
  *Why it matters:* Producers have [shut in about 63% of Gulf of Mexico oil output](https://apnews.com/article/hurricane-isaias-gulf-atlantic-alabama-florida-mississippi-8244ca45b8d4c292350f00adfabb0967), adding a domestic supply risk on top of the Middle East.
- **Jobless claims fall.** Initial jobless claims dropped to [197,000](https://oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/DataDashboard.asp), a sign the labor market remains firm.

## Compass Insight

Markets are caught in a tug-of-war between expectation and cost. AI enthusiasm has carried stocks to records, and Thursday's report didn't show lost sales — it questioned the yardstick. That a measurement dispute could knock chip stocks by more than 3% tells you how little margin for doubt is priced in. On the cost side, oil back above $100 and a 10-year Treasury yield still near 5.2% make it harder for the Fed to ease. In the short run, watch the Strait of Hormuz and Hurricane Isaias for oil. Over the longer run, the question is whether AI companies' reported earnings can justify the valuations investors have already paid for.
