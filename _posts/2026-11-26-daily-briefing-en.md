## Top U.S. Stories

- **Inflation picks up.** The Fed's preferred gauge, the [PCE price index](https://bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-october-2026), rose 3.8% from a year earlier in October. Core inflation rose to 3.3%, above [ING's 3.2% forecast](https://think.ing.com/downloads/pdf/article/strong-growth-and-elevated-inflation-signal-more-fed-tightening). A quarter-point Fed hike is widely expected at the [December 8–9 meeting](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomcminutes20261028.htm).
  *Why it matters:* Higher rates raise borrowing costs for mortgages and cards, and weigh on richly valued tech shares.
- **New tariffs proposed.** The U.S. Trade Representative [proposed Section 301 tariffs on 14 trading partners](https://www.ustr.gov/about/policy-offices/press-office/press-releases/2026/november/ustr-makes-determinations-and-proposes-actions-14-section-301-investigations-relating-structural), including up to 20% on covered Chinese goods, 15% for the EU, and up to 15% for Japan and South Korea. The proposal accounts for some existing duties and includes product exemptions. For Japan, [a separate option would raise that cap to 20%](https://ustr.gov/sites/default/files/files/Press/Releases/2026/Japan%20FRN%20Final.pdf) if talks or commitments fall short. The duties have not taken effect.
  *Why it matters:* Importers and exporters face fresh cost uncertainty, and the news added to risk-off trading.
- **Stocks recover most early losses.** The S&P 500 [fell as much as 3.2% intraday](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-ai-earnings-oil-10044b4fb55d3ff20fde9ba9e9acaab1) before closing 0.3% lower at 7,182.78. The Dow closed 0.3% higher at 50,182.95.
- **Hasan seeks a stay of execution.** Former Army psychiatrist Nidal Hasan, convicted over the 2009 Fort Hood attack, [has asked a judge to halt his execution scheduled for next week](https://www.investing.com/news/world-news/nidal-hasan-asks-us-judge-for-stay-of-execution-for-fort-hood-murders-5049402). Reuters reported that it would be the first U.S. military firing-squad execution since 1945 if it goes ahead.
- **Thanksgiving ritual.** President Trump [pardoned two turkeys, Freedom and Feathers](https://www.whitehouse.gov/thanksgiving/).
