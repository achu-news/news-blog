## Compass Insight

This week's markets are caught in a tug-of-war between diplomacy and interest rates. Trump's UN speech was harsh in tone, but it also gave a timeline for a deal with Iran, and hopes for reopening the Strait of Hormuz pulled crude down again. That is welcome relief for gasoline and heating bills. Borrowing costs, however, are still climbing: the Fed is hiking and signaling more, and the Bank of Japan just moved to 1.25%. That leaves the market dependent on the AI trade, which lifted the Nasdaq to another record while the rate-sensitive Dow slipped. In the near term, watch the Trump–Xi summit and whether oil breaks decisively below the $95–$100 zone. Longer term, the key question is whether easing energy prices can cool inflation enough to end the tightening cycle.
