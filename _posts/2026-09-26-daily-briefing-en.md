## Compass Insight

This week's message is simple: stocks are strong, but money is getting expensive. The 10-year Treasury yield is at levels last seen before the 2008 financial crisis, and Japan's long-term yields are near 30-year highs. Equities keep climbing anyway, supported by the AI investment boom and modest relief on U.S.-China trade and Middle East diplomacy. That combination is fragile. If Iran talks stall and oil pushes higher again, inflation fears could return quickly and pressure both bonds and stocks. In the near term, watch the October Fed meeting and any concrete progress on Hormuz. Over the longer run, the key question is whether 5%-plus yields become the new normal — which would reshape valuations, housing, and government budgets alike.
