## Compass Insight

Friday's selloff was less about one bad quarter than about a changing question: not "who is spending on AI?" but "who is getting paid for it?" Intel's weak outlook and Amazon's cash burn landed one day after Alphabet's slump, turning isolated disappointments into a theme. What makes this more delicate is the backdrop: oil is near $104 on Gulf shipping attacks, and the Fed leans toward raising rates, not cutting. In the short run, watch how Monday's Asian and European trading digests the Nasdaq drop and what the midterms mean for policy. Over the medium term, the test is whether AI leaders can show revenue growth that justifies their capital spending. Elevated oil plus rising rate expectations is a combination that can squeeze both household budgets and valuations.
