## Top U.S. Stories

- **Isaias slams the Gulf Coast.** The hurricane made [Category 2 landfall near Destin, Florida](https://www.wlox.com/2026/10/10/isaias-leaves-2-dead-widespread-outages-after-category-2-landfall-near-destin/) on Friday night — the first U.S. mainland hurricane landfall since Milton in 2024. As of Saturday, at least [four people had died in Florida and Alabama](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/hurricane-isaias-power-outage-9.7377047), and outages peaked near 900,000 before [dipping below 700,000](https://weather.com/2026/10/10/storms/hurricane/liveblogs/live-updates-isaias-pushes-across-southeast).

- **Washington sanctions the International Criminal Court itself.** The administration added the ICC to the Treasury's SDN list on October 9, [escalating from earlier sanctions on 13 judges and prosecutors](https://www.hrw.org/news/2026/10/09/us-sanctions-international-criminal-court). Secretary of State Marco Rubio [threatened to dismantle the court](https://www.kgou.org/politics-and-government/2026-10-10/u-s-imposes-sweeping-sanctions-on-international-criminal-court) unless it stops what Washington sees as a threat to Americans.
  *Why it matters:* Japan and seven other allies — every G7 member except the U.S. — [jointly said they "strongly oppose"](https://www.nikkei.com/article/DGXZQOUA100CI0Q6A011C2000000/) the move, a rare public split with Washington.

- **Powerful quake hits Panama.** A [magnitude 7.6 earthquake, later revised to 7.7](https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/09/americas/panama-earthquake-latam-intl), collapsed buildings near the epicenter on Friday; early reports showed no deaths, and the tsunami threat was lifted.
- **A heavy week ahead.** JPMorgan, Goldman Sachs, Citigroup and Wells Fargo report on Tuesday, followed by Morgan Stanley and Bank of America, and [September CPI arrives Wednesday](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-wall-streets-week-ahead-bank-earnings-cpi-headline-busy-markets-week/).
