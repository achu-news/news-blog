## Top U.S. Stories

- **Trump expects Iran talks to resume this week.** After rejecting Tehran's seven-day plan to reopen the Strait of Hormuz — "not the deal that I want to make" — President Trump said he expects negotiations to [resume in the coming week](https://www.cbsnews.com/live-updates/iran-war-us-trump-strait-of-hormuz-7-day-proposal/) and declined to rule out more strikes before the November midterms.
  *Why it matters:* The timeline for reopening Hormuz just slipped, which keeps upward pressure on oil, gasoline and inflation expectations.
- **Iran: "fully prepared" for war, but open to diplomacy.** Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi told NBC that Tehran had [not yet been formally notified](https://www.nbcnews.com/world/iran/iran-fully-prepared-war-resume-foreign-minister-rcna600059) of the rejection and that "it is up to President Trump to choose." Iran says it [won't soften its demands](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-27/iran-says-won-t-soften-demands-after-trump-rejects-hormuz-offer).
- **Nor'easter pounds the Northeast.** More than [130,000 customers lost power](https://www.nbcnews.com/weather/storms/noreaster-flooding-death-toll-rcna600078) Sunday morning, mostly along the New Jersey shore, Long Island and coastal Connecticut. Gusts hit 81 mph at Montauk Point, and a New York City housing worker was killed by a falling tree.
  *Why it matters:* Coastal flooding and outages could linger another day as the slow storm loops offshore.
- **Five arrested near U.S.-used air base in England.** UK police arrested five men near RAF Fairford on suspicion of explosives offenses and [preparing a terrorist act](https://www.gloucestershire.police.uk/news/gloucestershire/2026/september/counter-terrorism-policing-leading-investigation-following-whelford-arrests/). The base has hosted U.S. bombers for operations against Iran; investigators are [examining any Iran link](https://www.itv.com/news/2026-09-27/major-incident-declared-near-raf-fairford-as-residents-evacuated), which is not confirmed.
  *Why it matters:* It raises the risk that the Iran conflict spills into allied territory.
- **Hurricane Nolo threatens Hawaii.** A tropical storm warning and hurricane watch were issued for Hawaii Island, with officials warning of [flooding, mudslides and damaging surf](https://hta.hawaii.gov/alerts/hurricane-nolo-update/).
