- The United States and Denmark [reached a deal](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/19/g-s1-144158/us-and-denmark-reach-deal) on September 18 to build an expanded U.S. military presence in Greenland. President Trump said the agreement gives the U.S. "permanent control over security, and all other needs" on the island, while Danish PM Mette Frederiksen said it instead [reaffirms Danish sovereignty and Greenlandic self-determination](https://www.theweek.in/news/world/2026/09/19/us-and-denmark-describe-the-new-greenland-deal-in-strikingly-different-terms.html) — the two sides are describing the same deal in starkly different terms. *Why it matters:* a formal signing is planned at the UN General Assembly this week, but the deal still needs approval from both the Danish and Greenlandic parliaments before it takes effect, and Arctic security responsibilities are reportedly being shared with NATO rather than resting solely with Washington.
- A federal antitrust lawsuit filed September 18 in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California [accuses Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind and SpaceXAI](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/09/19/business/ai-slowdown-lawsuit-antitrust) of illegally coordinating to slow the pace of AI development. Four paying subscribers to AI services are the plaintiffs; the suit points to a September 12 essay by Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei calling for industry-wide deceleration on safety grounds, followed by public agreement from other AI labs' leaders. *Why it matters:* the case tests whether public statements about AI safety coordination can constitute an antitrust violation, with implications for how AI labs communicate about pacing future releases.
- An ICE officer [shot and injured a Venezuelan man](https://www.npr.org/2026/09/20/nx-s1-5975517/ice-shooting-austin-texas) during an operation in Austin, Texas on September 20. DHS said it is leading the investigation with the FBI; the man, identified as Wilber Rafael Garces Perez, 28, was reported in stable condition and in federal custody pending removal. *Why it matters:* it is at least the second ICE shooting in Texas this year, following a fatal shooting in Houston in July, and has drawn calls from Rep. Greg Casar for an independent investigation.
- Russian President Vladimir Putin signed a decree on September 17 [placing the Russian assets of Nestlé and French logistics firm FM Logistic](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/business/kremlin-seizes-nestle-assets-russia-intl) under the control of a newly formed company, LEV Management. The Kremlin linked the move to Western military support for Ukraine; Nestlé shares fell 2% in Zurich trading.
- Typhoon No. 25 slammed into Japan's Kanto region, with Izu Oshima island recording over 680mm of rain in 24 hours — roughly twice the average for the entire month of September. As of the morning of September 22, [two people (in their 50s and 60s) were confirmed dead and four remain missing](https://news.web.nhk/newsweb/na/nd-20260922de51778) in Chiba and Kanagawa prefectures, with search operations ongoing and evacuation orders issued to more than 1.9 million residents.
