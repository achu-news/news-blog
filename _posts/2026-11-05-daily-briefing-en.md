- Iran is [pushing for faster ceasefire talks](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/11/2/iran-pushing-accelerated-plan-for-ceasefire-talks-with-us), but no deal has been reached.

## Compass Insight

The rally is less about politics and more about oil. Markets read a Democratic Congress as raising the odds of a faster exit from the Iran war, which would lower energy prices and inflation pressure — a long-awaited relief with the 10-year Treasury yield still near [4.93%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/03/stock-market-today-live-updates.html). The picture is not uniform: AI and chip names slid on worries about tighter data-center rules, while consumer and bank stocks led. In the short run, watch whether oil holds below $100 and whether Iran talks move beyond words. Over the longer term, the question is how a divided Washington handles AI regulation, taxes, and spending. These are possibilities, not certainties.
