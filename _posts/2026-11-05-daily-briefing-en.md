## Compass Insight

The rally reflected both politics and oil. Wall Street often prefers divided government because it lowers the risk of large policy shifts, while falling oil eased inflation pressure and pushed the 10-year Treasury yield [down to 4.88% from 4.95%](https://apnews.com/article/stock-markets-ai-earnings-oil-4d12764c0b3ce38ffde3077712811182). The picture was not uniform: AI and chip names slid on worries about tighter data-center rules, while healthcare and low-price retailers rose. In the short run, watch whether oil holds below $100 and whether Iran talks move beyond words. Over the longer term, the question is how a divided Washington handles AI regulation, taxes, and spending. These are possibilities, not certainties.
