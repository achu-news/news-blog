## Compass Insight

Monday's market told one story: oil up, inflation fears up, yields up, stocks down. By rejecting Iran's offer, Trump signaled that a deal will come on his terms or not soon, and bond traders responded by pricing in a Fed that stays tight, or tightens further. Rate-sensitive tech lagged the Dow, a reminder that valuations built on low rates are the first to feel the squeeze. Note, too, that heavy government and AI-related bond issuance is adding to the pressure on yields independently of the Middle East. In the short term, watch whether this week's U.S.-Iran talks actually happen and Friday's jobs report. Over the longer run, the key question is how long households and companies can live with 5%-plus long-term rates.
