## Top U.S. Stories

- The U.S. economy added just [57,000 jobs in June, well short of the 113,000 expected](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/economy/june-jobs-report-stable-hiring-rcna352603), a worrying sign for the labor market as wage growth has tracked below inflation for a third straight month.
- Despite the soft jobs data, the Dow Jones Industrial Average [closed at a record high on July 2](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-2-2026), as investors bet the report would keep the Federal Reserve from tightening further.
- U.S. markets are [closed on Friday, July 3](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-2-2026) for the Independence Day holiday.
- U.S.-Iran indirect talks in Doha [paused ahead of the funeral of former Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/7/2/iran-war-live-positive-progress-as-us-tehran-wrap-up-indirect-talks?traffic_source=rss), which runs July 4–9. President Trump said talks had shown "positive progress."
- Iran's military warned [oil tankers transiting the Strait of Hormuz must use approved routes or face a "forceful response,"](https://www.cbc.ca/news/world/iran-oil-tankers-strait-of-hormuz-9.7255677?cmp=rss) keeping energy markets on edge.
- A record heat wave is expected to [make this Fourth of July the hottest on record for millions of Americans](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/heat-wave-hottest-july-4th-record-temperatures/), with more than 300 temperature records expected to fall by Saturday.

## Markets

Ahead of the holiday, the [Dow rose 1.14% to 52,900.07](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-2-2026), a fresh record, while the S&P 500 finished little changed and the Nasdaq Composite slipped as [Tesla and semiconductor stocks extended declines](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-july-2-223136955.html). The dollar softened against the yen after the weak jobs report, trading in a [160.6–161.8 yen range](https://www.gaitame.com/media/entry/2026/07/03/073945); the [frankfurter.app reference rate](https://api.frankfurter.app/latest?from=USD&to=JPY) put USD/JPY at 161.58 as of July 2.
